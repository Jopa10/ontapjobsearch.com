## Method

Only jobs already classified HIGH_CONFIDENCE or ELASTIC_FIT in the current admin/service register are assessed. A Manchester & Salford job is proposed for South Manchester only when its structured location, or a strong early-description location phrase, points to an area that the authoritative geo lookup already maps to Greater Manchester - South. This is intentionally conservative and does not infer from recruiter branch addresses or generic mentions.
