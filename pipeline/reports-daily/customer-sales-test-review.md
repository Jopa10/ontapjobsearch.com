## Working rule

**IN:** ordinary customer-facing selling carried out mainly by phone, contact centre, office, home or hybrid — including inbound/outbound sales, conversion, retention, renewals, upsell and internal/inside sales. Service Admin overlap is allowed.

**OUT:** field/door-to-door/event sales; self-employed/commission-only campaign work; senior/strategic sales or customer-success; specialist technical/product sales; pure customer service with no real sales responsibility; and obvious geo mismatches.
