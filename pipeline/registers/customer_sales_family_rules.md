# Customer Sales / Sales Advisor — governed proof rules

Status: **proof passed; national validation authorised; not LIVE**.

This file records the governed family boundary learned from the Hampshire, Greater Manchester - Manchester & Salford, and Yorkshire - West proof slices. It does not activate production publishing.

## Family definition

Customer Sales / Sales Advisor covers genuine sales-led office, contact-centre, home or hybrid roles where the worker primarily sells, converts, retains or renews customers.

Classification is non-exclusive. A legitimate Customer Sales job may also belong to Service Admin / Customer Service; crossover is not an exclusion reason.

## Direct sales titles

Sales Advisor/Adviser, Sales Executive, Sales Consultant, Sales Representative/Agent, Internal/Inside/Inbound/Outbound/Telephone Sales, Telesales, Retention/Renewals, New Business Executive/Advisor, Business Development Executive, Lead Generator, Appointment Setter and Membership Sales/Advisor are candidate titles.

A sales-led title is not sufficient by itself when the advert shows an excluded context.

## Customer/service crossover

Customer Service, Customer Care/Support, Customer Advisor/Representative, Client Service/Advisor, Contact/Call Centre and similar titles enter Customer Sales only when the advert contains genuine sales/conversion evidence such as commission, sales targets, upsell/cross-sell, conversion, warm leads/enquiries, outbound/inbound sales, new business, lead generation, closing, retention or renewals.

Pure service/admin remains outside Customer Sales.

## Account roles

Generic Account Manager / Account Executive titles are context-dependent, not automatic Customer Sales titles. They enter the diagnostic only when both are present:

1. strong commercial/sales evidence; and
2. office/digital/customer-led evidence such as office/hybrid/remote, phone, CRM, inbound/outbound or email.

Field, senior, strategic, enterprise/key/national/technical account roles and specialist-market account roles are excluded by default. Borderline specialist examples remain visible in national validation rather than being silently promoted to production eligibility.

## Hard exclusions

Exclude field/territory/area/regional or door-to-door sales; event/campaign/self-employed/commission-only selling; sales management/director roles; technical/product sales; retail/showroom/car/vehicle dealership sales; estate/lettings/sales negotiator roles; sales administration/support/ledger; automotive aftersales/service-advisor roles; and senior/specialist Customer Success roles.

Generic Sales Executive titles require advert-body refinement because the proof test showed the same title can describe either valid office sales or excluded automotive dealership sales.

## Geography QA

If a place embedded in the title maps to a different Ontap region from the routed job, exclude the row from that regional slice. The proof example was `Bournemouth- Sales Executive` routed to Manchester & Salford.

## Proof findings carried forward

- Greater Manchester - Manchester & Salford: 6 final jobs.
- Yorkshire - West: 6 final jobs.
