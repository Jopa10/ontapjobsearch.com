## Boundary frozen for this validation

### IN

- Legal Assistant / Legal Administrator / Legal Admin / Legal Secretary / Legal PA and equivalent legal-team support roles.
- Paralegals, including ordinary senior paralegal wording where there is no qualified-lawyer or management requirement.
- Paralegals who manage their own files/caseload under supervision or with normal occupational autonomy.
- Conveyancing Assistant / Conveyancing Administrator / Conveyancing Secretary / Conveyancing Paralegal.
- Probate Assistant / Probate Administrator / Probate Paralegal.
- Mixed `Paralegal / Fee Earner` wording remains eligible when the advert is substantively a paralegal-level opportunity rather than a management/qualified-lawyer role.
- Genuine legal-support roles may overlap with other Ontap families; overlap alone is not an exclusion.

### OUT

- Solicitor, barrister, lawyer, attorney, Legal Counsel / General Counsel, Legal Executive / licensed conveyancer and other qualified-lawyer boundaries.
- Partner, director, manager, Team Leader and departmental/team-lead roles.
- Standalone conveyancing Fee Earner titles where the title does not also identify the role as paralegal-level.
- Legal cashier / finance contamination, insurance claims-handler contamination and specialist titles already covered by the governed discovery config.
- Annualised salary maximum **over £50,000**. Exactly £50,000 is not excluded by salary alone.

### Advert-context principle

Owning files/caseloads or working with normal autonomy does **not** by itself make a paralegal OUT. The exclusion trigger is evidence that the role has crossed into qualified-lawyer, managerial, team/department-lead or otherwise senior-specialist territory.
