## Frozen occupational boundary

IN: practical in-house, agency, public-sector and charity Marketing/communications work whose core purpose is campaigns, content, social/digital channels, PR/media, brand, CRM/email, ecommerce, paid media or related delivery. Salary-appropriate assistant, administrator, coordinator, officer, executive, specialist, account and manager roles can qualify when the advert confirms that Marketing is the occupation.

OUT: pure sales/business development, finance support, field/self-employed promotion, training-course adverts, product management, Marketing/research analytics specialists, technical platform/data/engineering, government service content design, standalone creative production and senior corporate leadership. Explicit pay above £50,000 is OUT; description-only hourly/day/annual pay is annualised when JobG8 structured pay is blank.

OVERLAP: a genuine Marketing role may also fit Service Admin or Customer Sales. Overlap is not a reason to reject it. Mixed Marketing plus Sales/BD/Operations/Fundraising/Office titles require advert evidence that Marketing is a substantive part of the job.
